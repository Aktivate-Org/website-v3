Questions? Sorted.

Is it free to join?

Yes. Creating your account and your shop link costs nothing. A small fee is taken from payouts.

How many followers do I need?

You can start from 1,000 followers. Brands often prefer nano and micro creators because their audiences trust them.

How much can I earn from my shop?

Each brand sets the commission per product — between 10% and 50% of the sale, or a fixed amount for things like restaurants and hotels.

Do I need to handle deliveries?

No. Followers pay through secure Paystack checkout and the brand fulfils the order. You just share.

When do I get paid?

Campaign fees are released once your content is approved and live. Shop commission is paid 7 days after each purchase.

ShopLink FAQ

Who sets the commission?

Each brand sets it per product — anywhere from 10% to 50%. For things like restaurants and hotels, brands can pay a fixed amount per sale.

Does it cost anything?

No. Opening your shop is free for creators. Brands pay to list their products.

Can I still do brand campaigns?

Yes. Campaigns from the Collaboration Hub and your ShopLink sales live in the same account and pay into the same wallet.

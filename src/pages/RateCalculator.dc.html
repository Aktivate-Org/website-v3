Questions

How much should a nano influencer charge in Nigeria?

For one Instagram feed post, roughly ₦8,000–₦15,000 at 1,000 followers and ₦40,000–₦80,000 at 10,000 followers. Reels and TikToks sit higher, Stories lower. Strong engagement or a niche like tech or finance can add more.

What about micro influencers?

Between 10,000 and 50,000 followers, prices climb quickly: around ₦40,000–₦80,000 at 10K and ₦150,000–₦300,000 at 50K in Nigeria. Pick your country above to see your own currency.

Should I charge more for TikTok or Reels?

Usually yes. Short video takes longer to make and reaches more people who don’t follow you yet, so it’s typically priced 30–50% above a static post.

What are usage rights?

Permission for the brand to run your content as a paid ad or on its own pages. It’s extra value for them, so add it to your price (we use +30% for 30 days).

I have under 1,000 followers. Can I still earn?

Brands rarely pay cash below 1,000 followers, but you can still earn commission by recommending products with ShopLink while you grow.

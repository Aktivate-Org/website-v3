Questions? Sorted.

Do I need a website?

No. You can add products by hand in a few minutes. If you have a website, paste the link and we bring your products over.

Who delivers the orders?

You do, the way you deliver your own orders today. Every order comes to your dashboard and email with the buyer's details.

When do I get paid?

Your share of each sale is paid to your bank account the next morning. Payments are secure, by card, bank transfer or USSD.

Can I choose which creators sell my products?

Yes. Open your products to all eligible creators, approve each one yourself, or keep it invite only.

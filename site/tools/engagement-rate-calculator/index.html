What's your engagement rate?

Drop any Instagram or TikTok handle. We read the last 12 posts and tell you how engaged the audience really is — and how it compares to creators your size.

  • No login to Instagram
  • Public data only
  • Results in about 30 seconds
@

Engagement rate = average likes + comments (+ shares on TikTok) on recent posts, divided by followers.

How we work it out

  1. 1

    We read your last 12 posts

    Public likes, comments, shares and views. Pinned posts are skipped so old hits don't skew it.

  2. 2

    Divide by followers

    Average engagements per post ÷ followers × 100. The same formula brands use to shortlist creators.

  3. 3

    Compare to your tier

    Smaller accounts usually engage more, so we compare you with creators your size, not everyone.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. You can check up to 5 handles a day without signing up.

Do you need my password?

Never. We only read numbers anyone can see on a public profile. Private accounts can't be checked.

Why does engagement rate matter?

Brands care less about follower counts and more about whether people actually react. A strong rate is what gets nano and micro creators picked for paid campaigns.

My number is low. What can I do?

Post consistently, reply to comments in the first hour, ask questions in captions, and lean into the formats your audience already loves.

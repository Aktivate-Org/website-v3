Pricing questions

Can I pay in naira, shillings or cedis?

Yes. Prices show in naira, cedis or shillings based on where you are, and you can switch currency at the top of this page.

How do Aktivate Reports credits work?

One credit tracks one creator post. A pack of 50 credits costs ₦35,000 (or the local equivalent) and is valid for 30 days.

Is there a monthly subscription?

No. You pay per campaign, per business on ShopLink, or per report credit. Nothing recurring.

What does the 10% campaign fee cover?

Access to the creator network, applications and selection, and handling creator payouts.

Can I use Collaboration Hub and ShopLink together?

Yes — many businesses run a campaign for buzz, then list products so the same creators keep selling.

Black Friday is the one week a year your followers are actively looking for something to buy. So this week, we're paying you extra to sell to them.

**Make your first ShopLink sale of ₦15,000 or more between Monday 23 and Monday 30 November, and we'll add ₦10,000 to your earnings, on top of your normal commission.** Only the first 50 creators get it.

## What you get

Say you sell a ₦24,000 tote with 10% commission:

| | You earn |
|---|---|
| Your commission | ₦1,560 |
| Black Friday bonus | **₦10,000** |
| **Total from one sale** | **₦11,560** |

The bonus is the same whatever the product, as long as the order is ₦15,000 or more. One sale is all it takes.

## How to get it

1. **Share your store.** Put your ShopLink in your bio, then post it on your stories, WhatsApp status and anywhere your people hang out. Your link is `shop.useaktivate.com/@yourname`.
2. **Sell ₦15,000 or more.** One order of ₦15,000 or more, paid between 23 and 30 November (Lagos time).
3. **Get ₦10,000 extra.** Once the business delivers the order, the bonus lands in your ShopLink earnings and is paid out with your commission.

## The fine print, in plain words

- **One bonus per creator.** Your first qualifying sale earns it; sales after that earn your normal commission.
- **First 50 creators only.** When the spots are gone, they're gone. You can see how many are left on your Leaderboard page.
- **The order must be paid between Monday 23 November and the end of Monday 30 November, Lagos time.** It can be delivered after that.
- **Naira orders of ₦15,000 or more.** The minimum is the order total, so buying two or more of the same product counts.
- **Paid after delivery.** Like your commission, the bonus is held for 7 days in case of a refund, then it's yours.
- **Buying from your own store doesn't count.** Orders placed with your own email or phone number don't qualify, and a cancelled or refunded order loses the bonus.

## Where to see it

Open your ShopLink dashboard and tap **Leaderboard**. You'll see:

- whether you've earned your bonus, and how many spots are left
- the Black Friday week leaderboard, ranked by sales, with your place on it and how many more sales get you into the top 10

Your **My store** page shows the same thing at the top, so you always know where you stand.

## How to land your first sale this week

- **Pick 3 to 5 products with a real deal.** Shoppers are hunting discounts this week. Check the catalogue for products with Black Friday prices and put them at the top of your store.
- **Show it, don't just link it.** A 10-second video of you using the product beats a plain link every time. Say why you like it in one line.
- **Post on the big days.** Friday 27 November (Black Friday) and Monday 30 November (Cyber Monday) are when people buy. Post that morning and again in the evening.
- **Use WhatsApp.** Your status and close-friends groups convert better than anywhere else. People buy from people they know.
- **Pin it.** Pin your store link in your bio and keep a "My Black Friday picks" story highlight up all week.

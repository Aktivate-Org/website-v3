December is the one month when almost everyone you know is shopping for someone else. Gifts for your partner, your parents, your colleagues, the friends coming home for Detty December, the Secret Santa you drew at work.

Most of those people don't know what to buy. They scroll, they save posts, and they ask, "What should I get?"

That's where a creator with a good gift guide wins. You do the thinking for your followers, and if your picks are shoppable, you earn a commission every time someone buys one.

Here's how to do it.

## What a holiday gift guide actually is

A gift guide is a short, curated list of things you'd genuinely recommend as gifts, grouped in a way that makes it easy to choose: by person, by budget, or by vibe.

It works because it saves your followers time and removes the fear of buying the wrong thing. You're not just saying "this product is nice". You're saying "if you're buying for *this* person, at *this* budget, get *this*."

## Pick a clear angle

The best gift guides are specific. "Christmas gift ideas" is too broad. These work much better:

- **By budget:** gifts under ₦10,000, under ₦25,000, under ₦50,000, and "if you really love them"
- **By person:** for him, for her, for your mum, for the friend who has everything, for your office Secret Santa
- **By your niche:** a beauty creator's skincare gift guide, a tech creator's gadget guide, a foodie's guide to edible gifts and hampers, a home creator's guide to gifts for a new apartment
- **By moment:** gifts for the people flying in for Detty December, last-minute gifts that still arrive before Christmas, gifts for the January birthdays

Stick to your niche. Your followers trust you on the things you usually talk about, so a beauty creator recommending power banks won't convert the way a beauty creator recommending a skincare set will.

## Choose products you'd stand behind

Your recommendations are only worth something because your followers trust them. Protect that.

- Recommend things you've used, tried or seen up close. If you haven't, say so.
- Mix price points so everyone can buy something.
- Favour products that can actually be delivered in time. Check delivery times and cut-off dates, especially for anything shipping from outside Lagos or Abuja.
- Include a few Nigerian brands. People love discovering local businesses, and many of them are happy to work with creators.

Five to ten products per guide is plenty. More than that and people stop reading.

## Format it for each platform

You don't need one perfect post. You need the same guide in a few formats:

- **Carousel (Instagram):** one product per slide, with the price and who it's for. These get saved and shared, which is exactly what you want in December.
- **Reel or TikTok:** a fast "gifts under ₦25k" video showing each product in your hands, with prices on screen.
- **Stories:** one product per story with a link sticker, then save them to a "Gift guide" highlight so people can find it all month.
- **WhatsApp status and channel:** your followers on WhatsApp are often your warmest buyers. Post the guide there too.
- **Pin it:** pin the carousel or video to the top of your profile for December.

## Make every pick shoppable

A gift guide with no links leaves your followers to go and search for each product themselves, and many won't bother. Make it one tap from "I like that" to "bought".

Every product in your gift guide sits in one place, your followers can buy straight from your store, and you earn a commission on every sale. There's no stock to hold and no deliveries to handle: the brand ships the order.

Tip: name your guide in your store and in your captions the same way ("Gifts under ₦25k") so people know exactly where to tap.

## Post at the right time

December shopping happens in waves. Plan for all of them:

1. **Late November to the first week of December:** post your main guide. Early planners and people buying for family abroad shop now.
2. **Mid-December:** repost with fresh picks and a reminder, as salaries and end-of-year bonuses land.
3. **Around 18 to 22 December:** post a "last-minute gifts that still arrive in time" guide. Check delivery cut-offs first.
4. **Between Christmas and New Year:** gift cards, experiences and "treat yourself" picks for people spending their own gift money.

## Be clear when you earn

If you earn a commission or were paid to include a product, tell your followers. A simple "I earn a commission if you buy through my store" or "#ad" keeps their trust, and in Nigeria, advertising rules expect sponsored and affiliate content to be disclosed.

Honesty also sells. Followers who know you'd only recommend things you believe in are far more likely to buy.

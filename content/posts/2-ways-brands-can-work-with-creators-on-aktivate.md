Working with creators shouldn't mean chasing people in DMs, guessing what to pay, and never finding out what you got for your money.

[Aktivate](https://useaktivate.com) gives brands two ways to work with creators across Africa. One is for paid campaigns, when you want specific content on specific dates. The other is for sales, when you want creators to sell your products and you only pay when they do.

Here's how each works and when to use it.

## Way 1: Run paid campaigns on the Collaboration Hub

The [Collaboration Hub](https://app.useaktivate.com) is where you post a campaign and have verified creators apply to work with you.

**How it works**

1. **Post your campaign.** Describe your brand, what you want creators to make (Reels, TikToks, stories, UGC videos), your budget, the dates and who you're looking for.
2. **Creators apply.** Verified creators who fit your brief apply, with their profile, audience and engagement visible to you.
3. **Pick who you want.** Accept the creators who fit, and decline the rest.
4. **Review and approve.** Creators send their drafts for you to check before anything goes live.
5. **See the results.** Posts go live, and you see the links and performance in one place.

**Use it when you want:**

- Content on a specific date, such as a launch, an event or a sale
- A specific message, script or format
- UGC videos to use in your own ads
- Awareness and reach in a particular city or audience

## Way 2: Let creators sell for you on ShopLink

[ShopLink](https://shop.useaktivate.com) is a creator storefront built for Africa. Instead of paying creators for posts, you pay them a commission when they sell.

**How it works**

1. **List your products.** Add them yourself, or import them from your Bumpa, Shopify or Catlog store, your website, or a spreadsheet.
2. **Set the commission:** anywhere from 5% to 50% of the price. For services like restaurants, hotels and events, you can set a fixed amount per sale instead.
3. **Creators add your products to their stores.** They share their ShopLink store with their followers.
4. **Shoppers buy.** They pay securely, and your share goes straight to your bank account. The creator earns their commission.
5. **You see who sold.** Every order shows which creator brought it, so you know who really moves your products.

There's a one-time activation fee per business ($15, or ₦15,000 in Nigeria). After that, you pay only when something sells.

**Use it when you want:**

- Sales, with no upfront fees to creators
- To work with many creators at once without managing each one
- To find out which creators actually sell for you
- An always-on sales channel, not a one-off campaign

## Collaboration Hub vs ShopLink at a glance

| | Collaboration Hub | ShopLink |
|---|---|---|
| **You pay for** | The content | The sales |
| **When you pay** | Per campaign | Only when someone buys |
| **Best for** | Launches, events, UGC, awareness | Ongoing sales of products and bookings |
| **Control** | Your brief, your approval | Your commission, your products |
| **Results you see** | Posts, reach, engagement | Orders, revenue and sales by creator |

## Why most brands use both

The two work best together:

1. **Use ShopLink to find your sellers.** Let creators add your products and see who actually drives sales.
2. **Use the Collaboration Hub to reward them.** Book the creators who sell for paid campaigns around launches, Black Friday or December.
3. **Repeat.** Your paid budget goes to creators with proven results, while ShopLink keeps sales coming in between campaigns.

## Tips for getting the best results

- **Pick creators by fit, not followers.** Smaller creators with the right audience often sell more than big names.
- **Be generous with commission on your best products.** Creators prioritise products that are worth sharing.
- **Give creators what they need:** good product photos, clear prices and a short description of who it's for.
- **Reply fast** to creators and customers.
- **Measure and rebook.** Keep working with the creators who deliver.

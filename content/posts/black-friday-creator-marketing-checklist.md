Black Friday has become one of the biggest shopping moments in Nigeria and across Africa. Shoppers wait for it, compare deals, and buy from the brands they trust.

Creators are one of the best ways to be part of that conversation. A recommendation from someone your customers follow cuts through a feed full of discount flyers. But a Black Friday creator campaign only works if the offer, the creators, the timing and the tracking are all in place.

Use this checklist to get it right, whether you're planning weeks ahead or pulling it together in a few days. Black Friday 2026 is on **Friday, 27 November**, and Cyber Monday on **Monday, 30 November**.

## 1. Get your offer ready

- [ ] **Decide the deal:** a percentage off, a bundle, free delivery, or a gift with purchase. Make it simple enough to explain in one sentence.
- [ ] **Make it real.** Shoppers notice when prices go up before a "discount". Fake deals damage trust, and consumer protection rules apply.
- [ ] **Set the dates:** Black Friday only, the whole week, or through Cyber Monday.
- [ ] **Check your stock** for the products you're pushing.
- [ ] **Prepare for more orders:** packaging, delivery partners, payment and customer service.

## 2. Choose the right creators

- [ ] **Pick by fit, not followers.** Choose creators whose audience matches your customers in age, location and interests.
- [ ] **Mix sizes:** a few micro creators for reach, plus several nano creators for trust and volume.
- [ ] **Check engagement** before you commit, with our free [engagement rate calculator](/tools/engagement-rate-calculator/).
- [ ] **Check budgets** against typical [creator rates in Nigeria](/tools/influencer-rate-calculator/?country=NG&mode=brand).
- [ ] **Book early.** Good creators get busy in late November and December.

## 3. Write a clear brief

Your brief should fit on one page:

- [ ] The product and the exact offer (with the code and dates)
- [ ] The key message in one sentence
- [ ] What to post: format, number of posts and stories, and the posting dates
- [ ] What to show: the product in use, the price before and after, the deadline
- [ ] The link or code to share
- [ ] Disclosure: #ad or the platform's paid partnership label
- [ ] Whether you'll approve content before it goes live, and by when
- [ ] Payment amount, terms and timing

## 4. Set up tracking

If you can't see which creator drove which sale, you can't learn anything for next time.

- [ ] **A unique code for each creator:** TOLU20, ADA20
- [ ] **A unique link for each creator**
- [ ] **"How did you hear about us?"** at checkout or in your DMs
- [ ] **A simple sheet** to record each creator's posts, clicks, orders and revenue

## 5. Plan the posting schedule

Black Friday isn't one day. Plan for the whole week:

- [ ] **The week before:** teaser posts ("Something big is coming Friday") and "add to wishlist" content
- [ ] **Black Friday morning:** the main post, with the offer and the code, live early in the day
- [ ] **Through the weekend:** stories with reminders, stock updates and customer reactions
- [ ] **Cyber Monday:** a "last chance" push
- [ ] **Afterwards:** extend into December with gift guides

## 6. Pay for results where you can

Flat fees for every creator add up quickly, and not every post will sell. Paying commission ties your cost directly to sales.

- [ ] **Consider commission for some or all creators.** On [ShopLink](https://shop.useaktivate.com), you list your products and set your commission. Creators add them to their own store and share them, you pay only when someone buys, and every order shows which creator brought it.
- [ ] **Use fees for content you need on a specific date**, and post the campaign on the [Aktivate Collaboration Hub](https://app.useaktivate.com) so verified creators can apply.

## 7. During the weekend

- [ ] **Reshare creator posts** on your own pages and stories
- [ ] **Reply fast** to comments and DMs, because slow replies lose Black Friday sales
- [ ] **Watch your codes and links:** put more effort behind the creators who are selling
- [ ] **Update creators on stock** so nobody promotes something that's sold out

## 8. After the weekend

- [ ] **Pay creators on time.** It's the easiest way to get them back next time.
- [ ] **Work out results per creator:** orders, revenue, cost per sale and ROI.
- [ ] **Rebook the creators who sold** for your December campaign
- [ ] **Ask for usage rights** on the best-performing content so you can use it in ads
- [ ] **Write down what you learned** for next year

## Running late?

If Black Friday is days away, keep it simple: one clear offer, three to five creators who already fit your brand, one code each, and posts going live on Friday morning. A small, well-tracked campaign beats a big, messy one.

Good luck, and may your stock sell out for the right reasons.

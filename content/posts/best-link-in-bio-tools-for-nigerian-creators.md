Instagram and TikTok give you one link in your bio. For most creators, that single link is where followers go to find your content, buy what you recommend, book you, or pay for what you sell.

A link in bio tool turns that one link into a page with everything on it. But the tools aren't all built for the same job, and several popular ones were designed for creators in the US, not Nigeria.

Here's how the main options compare, and how to pick the right one for what you actually want to do.

## First, decide what your link is for

Before choosing a tool, be clear about the main job of your bio link:

1. **Sending people to your content and socials:** YouTube, podcast, other accounts
2. **Selling your own digital products:** ebooks, courses, templates, presets, coaching
3. **Earning from products you recommend:** commission on what your followers buy
4. **Getting brand deals and bookings:** a media kit and a way to contact you

Most creators need a mix, but one of these is usually the priority. That decides which tool fits best.

## The tools compared

### Linktree

The best-known link in bio tool. Quick to set up, with a clean page of buttons linking out to anything.

- **Good for:** a simple page of links to your content and socials
- **Selling:** it can sell products and take payments, but selling features vary by plan and come with fees, and checkout is built around international payment options
- **Watch for:** fees on sales on some plans, and Linktree branding on the free plan
- **Cost:** free plan available, with paid plans charged monthly (prices vary by country)

### Beacons

A link in bio page with built-in tools for creators: a store for digital products, email collection and a media kit.

- **Good for:** creators who want an all-in-one page with a media kit for brands
- **Selling:** sells digital products, with a percentage fee on sales on the free plan
- **Watch for:** the seller fee on the free plan, and payment options built mainly for international buyers
- **Cost:** free plan available, paid plans monthly

### Stan Store

A storefront designed as a link in bio, popular with coaches and course creators in the US.

- **Good for:** selling courses, coaching calls and digital products to an international audience
- **Selling:** strong checkout for digital products and bookings, with no transaction fee on its paid plans
- **Watch for:** there's no free plan (paid plans start at about $29 a month), and it's priced in dollars
- **Cost:** monthly subscription only

### Selar

A Nigerian platform for selling digital products, courses, memberships and coaching, used widely by creators across Africa.

- **Good for:** selling your own digital products to Nigerian and African buyers
- **Selling:** buyers can pay in naira using local options like cards, bank transfer and USSD, and it also supports international pricing
- **Watch for:** it's built for selling your own products, not as a page of links or a way to earn from other brands' products
- **Cost:** free to start, with a fee on sales

### ShopLink by Aktivate

A creator storefront built for Africa, where you add products from brands to your own store and earn commission on every sale.

- **Good for:** earning from the products you already recommend, like beauty, fashion, food, gadgets and home items
- **Selling:** brands list their products and set the commission. You add the ones you like to your store and share one link. Your followers buy straight from your store, pay securely in naira, and the brand handles delivery
- **Watch for:** it's for selling brands' products on commission, so pair it with a tool like Selar if you also sell your own courses or ebooks
- **Cost:** free for creators

## Quick comparison

| Tool | Best for | Free plan | Naira payments | Earn from brands' products |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Linktree** | A simple page of links | Yes | Limited | Limited |
| **Beacons** | All-in-one page with media kit | Yes | Limited | Limited |
| **Stan Store** | Courses and coaching for international buyers | No | No | No |
| **Selar** | Selling your own digital products in Africa | Yes | Yes | No (you can be an affiliate for other Selar sellers) |
| **ShopLink** | Earning commission on products you recommend | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Prices and features change often, so check each tool's own pricing page before you sign up.

## Which should you choose?

- **You mainly share content:** Linktree or Beacons is enough.
- **You sell your own ebook, course or coaching in Nigeria:** Selar is built for that.
- **You sell courses to an international audience and can pay monthly in dollars:** Stan Store.
- **You recommend products and want to earn from them:** ShopLink.
- **You do a bit of everything:** use one tool as your main page and link to the others from it. For example, put your ShopLink store at the top of a Linktree or Beacons page, followed by your Selar products and contact details.

## Tips for a bio link that actually converts

1. **Put the most important thing first.** Most people only tap the top one or two buttons.
2. **Name buttons clearly:** "Shop my skincare picks" beats "My store".
3. **Keep it short.** Five or six links is plenty.
4. **Update it when you post.** If today's Reel is about a product, make sure it's at the top.
5. **Tell people to tap it.** "Link in bio" still works, especially in stories and at the end of videos.
6. **Check your clicks** every week and remove what nobody uses.

Your bio link is the one place every follower can act on what you post. Make sure it's working as hard as your content does.

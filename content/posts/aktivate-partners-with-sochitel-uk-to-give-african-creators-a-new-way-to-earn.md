Some partnerships are good for business; others mean something. This one is both.

Aktivate has officially partnered with Sochitel UK Limited, and the reason this one hits different is that Sochitel was the first brand to sign up for ShopLink, our affiliate commerce product built for African creators. Long before the formal announcement, this was already a relationship we'd been building from the ground up.

* * *

## What Is Sochitel?

Sochitel is a global digital goods distributor that gives consumers access to airtime, mobile data, eSIMs, and international vouchers across multiple markets. For African audiences — many of whom rely on prepaid mobile networks and cross-border digital products — Sochitel's catalogue is genuinely useful.

That's the keyword: useful. The best affiliate products are the ones an audience was already going to buy. Sochitel sits squarely in that category.

* * *

## What This Means for Creators on Aktivate

Through this partnership, creators on Aktivate can now earn royalties every time their audience purchases a digital product through their unique ShopLink. The product catalogue includes:

*   **Airtime** — across multiple African networks

*   **Mobile data bundles**

*   **eSIMs** — for creators and audiences that travel

*   **International vouchers**

*   **OneFootball Weekly Pass Vouchers**


That last one deserves its own mention.

* * *

## The OneFootball Angle

If you understand African audiences, you know football isn't just entertainment — it's culture. Matchday is a collective experience. Conversations about the Champions League, AFCON, and the Premier League run across every social platform, every WhatsApp group, every comment section.

OneFootball's Weekly Pass gives fans access to premium football content at a discount. Through ShopLink, creators can now embed that into their content naturally — not as a sponsored post, not as a one-off deal, but as a product their audience genuinely wants, attached to a link that earns them money every time someone buys.

That's a different kind of brand deal. That's infrastructure.

* * *

## Why This Is Bigger Than a Partnership Announcement

The dominant model for creator income in Africa right now is brand campaigns — a brand pays a creator to post, the post goes live, the campaign ends. The creator moves on to the next brief. The income is episodic and unpredictable.

ShopLink is built to change that. The idea is simple: a creator publishes content, someone in their audience buys a product through their link, the creator earns a royalty. That royalty doesn't expire when the campaign ends. It keeps working as long as the content is live and the audience keeps buying.

Recurring income. From content that's already been made.

The Sochitel partnership is the clearest example yet of what that looks like in practice — a catalogue of products that African audiences already purchase regularly, now attached to creator links that generate ongoing earnings.
